Our Story

Recipe Secrets Restaurant began as a small collection of handwritten family recipes, passed down through three generations. In 2013 we opened our doors with a simple goal: cook honest food, source it responsibly, and make every guest feel like family.

Today, our menu still carries those original recipes — reinterpreted each season with the best produce we can find from local farms and markets.

Our Philosophy

We believe great food starts with great ingredients. We work directly with local farmers and fishmongers, change our menu with the seasons, and waste as little as possible. Every dish is made from scratch, every day, because secrets worth keeping are worth doing properly.
